IDEAS BRAINSTORMED
OL:    1 Pocket Blame Map (chosen)  2 Anchor Score  3 Line Chemistry Matrix
Edge:  4 Bend Index  5 Gravity / Teammate Pressure Lift
QB:    6 Pocket Feel Score  7 Clock Left on the Table
Other: 8 Chip Value (RB/TE)  9 Disguise Score (safeties/corners)

CHOSEN: POCKET ACCOUNTABILITY MAP (originally "Pocket Blame Map")
Name: it assigns responsibility for lost pocket space to a specific blocker,
frame by frame. Renamed to sound less negative to coaches.
Metric: Square Yards Surrendered (SYS).
Method:
 1. Every 0.1s, sample a 5-yard-radius disk around the QB on a 0.25-yd grid.
 2. A grid point is lost when a pass rusher is closer to it than the QB.
    It belongs to the nearest rusher.
 3. Subtract each rusher's area at the snap (alignment isn't charged); clip at 0.
 4. Charge that area to the rusher's blocker (from PFF); split it when he is
    double-teamed. Unblocked rushers go to "UNBLOCKED".
 5. Headline number: SYS at 2.5s (plays lasting at least 2.5s). Also recorded:
    at the end of the play, the peak, and the average over the play.

PLAN: THREE WORKSTREAMS, ALL RUNNING AT THE SAME TIME
A) Metric & pipeline: rename the folder to pocket_accountability/; fix edge
   cases (missing frames, scrambles, helper blockers); add positions; check
   radius sensitivity (4/5/6 yd; keep 5 unless Spearman rho < 0.85); export
   leaderboard.json (min ~150 snaps) and replays.json (normalised so offense
   moves up the screen, LOS at y=0). Ship a stub replays.json with one play first.
B) Validation & insights: does SYS track PFF sacks/hits/hurries allowed (AUC)?
   Is it stable odd vs even weeks, compared with pressure rate? Find "hidden
   leaky" / "unfairly blamed" linemen; context splits (dropBackType, play action,
   rusher count); pick 4-6 showcase plays (obvious-culprit sack, surprise-culprit
   sack, clean pocket, stunt/SW). Starts on the v1 CSV now.
C) Visualisation & pitch: rename the page; load real replays from JSON with a
   play picker; real leaderboard with search and per-player panel; a "Does it
   work?" section; coach/scout/broadcaster angles; slides plus a 2-minute demo.
Handoffs, each with a stand-in until it arrives:
  A->C data format/JSON (stub file), B->A showcase plays (any sack play),
  B->C headline numbers (placeholders).
Critical path: A's leaderboard.json. Fallback: build it from the v1 CSV.

VERIFICATION

  charged blocker should have the highest SYS on that play.
- Validation: SYS clearly higher on pressure-allowed plays; report odd/even-week
  correlation whatever the result.
- Page: node --check on the extracted script; slider, bars and tooltip agree on
  each showcase play; check light/dark/phone width; republish.
