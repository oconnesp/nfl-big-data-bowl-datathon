# Pocket Accountability Map: 2-minute demo

About 290 spoken words at roughly 150 words a minute. Numbers in [brackets] are placeholders until Workstream B delivers.

## 0:00–0:25 · What SYS is
**On screen:** page top, play picker on "ATL vs PHI · Q4 4th & 10 · Sack", slider at 0.0s.
> Sacks and pressures tell you the pocket collapsed. They don't tell you who gave up the ground, or how much. This is the Pocket Accountability Map. Every tenth of a second we draw a five-yard pocket around the quarterback. Any spot a rusher reaches first is lost, and it's charged to the lineman PFF says was blocking him. We call it Square Yards Surrendered, and the headline number is SYS at two and a half seconds.

## 0:25–0:55 · A real sack
**On screen:** press Play; pause at 2.5s (the 2.5s tick); then drag to the end (Sack).
> A real play: Atlanta, fourth and ten, Matt Ryan sacked by Javon Hargrave. Each colour is a blocker. PFF charged the sack to the left guard, Jalen Mayfield, and at two and a half seconds he leads with about seven and a half square yards. Keep playing: by the sack, the center's man has taken almost eighteen. One number, two people responsible.

## 0:55–1:10 · Coach
**On screen:** Coach card, "Show the worst rep"; the player panel opens (weekly chart, worst rep).
> For a coach, the question is which rep goes on film. One click opens the lineman who surrendered the most space, his week-by-week trend, and his single worst rep with the game situation.

## 1:10–1:25 · Scout
**On screen:** close the panel; Scout card, "Search the leaderboard"; type a team; tap Guards.
> For a scout, every lineman is ranked against his own position: SYS at two and a half seconds, the position average, a percentile and a tier, from Wall to Leaky.

## 1:25–1:40 · Broadcaster
**On screen:** Broadcaster card, "Play broadcast view"; let the replay run.
> For a broadcaster, flip to broadcast view: one outlined pocket, a big number counting down the space left, and the name of the lineman giving it up. Built for the replay.

## 1:40–2:00 · Does it work, and close
**On screen:** scroll to "Does it work?".
> We measured eight thousand five hundred dropbacks from the first eight weeks of 2021. SYS separates snaps where PFF charged a pressure with an AUC of [0.xx], and it's [more / less] stable week to week than pressure rate. Pressures count the damage. The Pocket Accountability Map shows where it came from.
