# Pocket Accountability Map: 2-minute demo

About 300 spoken words at roughly 150 words a minute. Every number below matches the data shipped in `web/public/data/`.

## 0:00–0:20 · What SYS is
**On screen:** top of the page; play picker on "SYS disagrees with PFF: BUF vs PIT · Q4 1st & 10 · Sack"; slider at 0.0s.
> Sacks and pressures tell you the pocket collapsed. They don't tell you who gave up the ground. This is the Pocket Accountability Map. Every tenth of a second we draw a five-yard pocket around the quarterback. Any spot a rusher reaches first is lost, and it's charged to the lineman PFF says was blocking him. That's Square Yards Surrendered. The headline number is SYS at two and a half seconds.

## 0:20–0:50 · A real sack
**On screen:** press Play; pause at 1.6s; hover the shaded space on the left side.
> Buffalo, fourth quarter, Josh Allen sacked by T.J. Watt. PFF charged the sack to the right guard. Watch the left side. By one point six seconds, left tackle Dion Dawkins has given up over thirty square yards, and the pocket is down from sixty to sixteen. Dawkins was flagged for holding on this play. The map caught it from the tracking data alone.

## 0:50–1:05 · Coach
**On screen:** Coach card → "Show the worst rep"; the player panel opens (SYS by week, worst rep); "Watch his showcase rep".
> For a coach, the question is which rep goes on film. One click opens the lineman giving up the most space against his position, Penei Sewell here, with his week-by-week trend and the rep to pull up.

## 1:05–1:20 · Scout
**On screen:** close the panel; Scout card → "Search the leaderboard"; type "BUF"; tap Tackles.
> For a scout, all ninety-two qualified linemen are ranked within their own position, with the position average, a percentile and a tier from Wall to Leaky.

## 1:20–1:35 · Broadcaster
**On screen:** Broadcaster card → "Play broadcast view"; let it run.
> For a broadcaster, one outlined pocket, a big number counting down the space left, and the name of the lineman giving it up. Ready for the replay.

## 1:35–2:00 · Does it work, and close
**On screen:** scroll to "Does it work?".
> We measured eight thousand five hundred dropbacks. SYS separates snaps where PFF charged a pressure with an AUC of zero point seven two, and mean SYS climbs from three point six on clean snaps to nine and a half on sacks. It's as stable week to week as pressure rate, and the ranking doesn't move if you change the pocket size. Pressures count the damage. The Pocket Accountability Map shows where it came from.
