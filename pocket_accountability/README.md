The Pocket Accountability Map introduces *Square Yards Surrendered (SYS)*, a new offensive-line metric that measures pass protection in field space instead of sacks and pressures. Every tenth of a second, we split a 5-yard pocket around the quarterback between the QB and the pass rushers, then charge any ground a rusher gains after the snap to the lineman PFF assigned to block him (split on double teams, "Unblocked" for free rushers). Across 8,532 dropbacks from the 2021 Big Data Bowl tracking data (weeks 1–8), SYS separates snaps where PFF charged a lineman with a pressure (AUC 0.72), climbs from 3.6 yd² on clean snaps to 9.5 yd² on sacks, and gives the same lineman rankings whether the pocket is 4, 5 or 6 yards. The interactive web app replays showcase plays with lost space shaded in the colour of the responsible blocker, ranks 92 qualified linemen within their position, and serves coaches (which rep to put on film), scouts (where a lineman ranks against his position) and broadcasters (a live pocket-shrinking overlay).
