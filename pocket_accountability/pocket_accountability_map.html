Pocket Accountability Map

Each frame, the QB's pocket is the space he can reach before any rusher. When a rusher takes some of it, the blocker assigned to that rusher is charged. The result is Square Yards Surrendered (SYS): a protection stat for each lineman, measured in field space instead of sacks and pressures.

Does it work?

"Top-SYS blocker" = on plays where PFF charged a blocker, how often he is also the blocker who surrendered the most pocket space (peak SYS), vs. picking a blocker at random. AUC: chance a PFF-flagged blocker-play has higher SYS than an unflagged one. SYS and PFF disagree on about half of sacks, and some of those look like SYS is right. See the hidden-culprit play.

Who it's for

Coach

Not just "whose sack was it?". Which lineman leaks space on every rep, and the exact snap to pull up on film (Worst rep).

Scout

A lineman's percentile against his own slot, built from every pass-pro rep rather than the handful that end in pressure, and stable whether the pocket is 4, 5 or 6 yards (ρ 0.99).

Broadcaster

A live overlay that shows the pocket shrinking and lights up the guilty blocker before the sack happens.

1 · Draw the pocket

Sample a 5-yard disk around the QB every 0.1s. A point belongs to the QB if he is closer to it than every pass rusher.

2 · Find who took it

Points a rusher is closer to are lost to him. Subtract what he owned at the snap, so only ground gained after the snap counts.

3 · Charge the blocker

PFF's block assignments link each rusher to his blocker. Doubles split the charge. Free rushers go to "Unblocked", which points at the scheme or a missed call.
